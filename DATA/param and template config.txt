            1. Debt Ratio

            3. Debt Equity Ratio (DER)

                netSales / ( totalCurrentAssets - totalCurrentLiabilities )
            5. Operating Revenue To External Liabilities
                operatingRevenue / externalLiabilities
            6. Net Profit Margin
                (netProfit / revenue) * 100
            7. Current Ratio
                totalCurrentAssets / totalCurrentLiabilities
            8. Affiliates Due to Net Worth Ratio
