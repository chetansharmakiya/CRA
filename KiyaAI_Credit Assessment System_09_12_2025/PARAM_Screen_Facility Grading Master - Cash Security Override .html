Configure cash collateral coverage thresholds for automatic rating overrides as per Credit Risk Management Policy V19, Page 55.

Cash Security Override Policy

  • Purpose: When credit facilities are fully backed by cash collateral, the system forces specific grades regardless of calculated rating.
  • Grade 1 (≥110%): Cash collateral at or above 110% forces ORR to Grade 1 (Highest Credit Quality).
  • Grade 2 (100%-110%): Cash collateral between 100% and 110% forces ORR to Grade 2 (Strong Credit Quality).
  • Below 100%: No override applied - system retains the calculated grade.
  • Override Priority: Cash security override takes precedence over all other adjustments in STEP 6B.
