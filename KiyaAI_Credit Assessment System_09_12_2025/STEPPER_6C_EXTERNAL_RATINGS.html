Step 6C: External Rating Selection & Override

Select between system-calculated rating or externally mapped rating as per Credit Risk Management Policy V19

External Rating Override Logic

When an entity has published credit ratings from recognized agencies (S&P, Moody's, Fitch), you can choose to override the system-calculated rating with the externally mapped grade. The selected rating cannot exceed (be better than) the external rating grade. This ensures regulatory compliance and conservative risk assessment.
